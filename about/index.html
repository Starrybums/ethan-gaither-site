About

One person, start to finish

I build websites by hand in Battle Ground, Washington.

Who I am

Hi, I'm Ethan

I'm Ethan Gaither. I live in Battle Ground, Washington, and I build websites for small businesses around here.

I write the pages by hand. I use AI tools like Claude Code, Codex and Cursor to get them done faster, and I read and test what they produce before anything goes live. You deal with me from the first call to the handover. There's no sales rep and no hand-off to a team.

Before this I built an agency site, a booking system and a few spec builds for my own business, Blackline Studios. That's Blackline work, and I label it that way wherever it shows up.

How I work

Four steps

  1. Listen

    We talk about the business, who needs to find you, and what a visitor should do on the site.

  2. Agree

    You get a fixed price and a plain list of what's in and what's out, before I start.

  3. Build and show

    I build it by hand and show you a working draft you can open on your phone.

  4. Hand over

    The domain, hosting, code and logins go in your name. For 14 days after launch I fix anything that's off.

The full version, step by step, is on the services page.

What I care about

Three things

  • You own it

    Your domain, your hosting account, your code and your logins. If we stop working together, your site keeps running.

  • Plain language

    No jargon in the quote and none in the emails. If I use a word you don't know, ask and I'll explain it.

  • Speed

    Pages that load fast, because people leave slow sites. And a project that moves: about four weeks when your content is ready.

Tools

What I use

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the site itself. Vercel and GitHub to host it and keep its history. Claude Code, Codex and Cursor to build faster. Lighthouse to test it.

There's no page builder, no framework and no plugins to keep updated. Anyone who knows the web can pick the site up later.
