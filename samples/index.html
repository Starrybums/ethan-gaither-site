Every one is made up

The businesses, names, addresses and prices are invented. The phone numbers use the 555-01xx range, which is set aside for fiction, so none of them can ring a real shop.

Each sample says so at the top and at the bottom, and is hidden from search engines. There are no stock photos. The art is drawn or built from patterns.

They look different from each other on purpose. A plumber and a clothing label shouldn't look alike. The habits underneath are the same: built for phones first, easy to read, and usable with a keyboard.
