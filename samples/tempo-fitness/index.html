Classes

Pick your pace.

Five classes, each coached from start to finish. Every move has an easier and a harder version, so a first-timer and a regular can share a room.

  • Tempo Intervals

    45 minIntensity 5 of 5

    Hard rounds, short rests, all timed to the music. Kettlebells, dumbbells and your own weight.

  • Barbell Basics

    60 minIntensity 3 of 5

    Squat, hinge, press and pull with a coach watching your form. Start light. Add weight when it feels smooth.

  • Row + Run

    45 minIntensity 4 of 5

    Rowing machines and treadmills in short rounds. You choose the pace for each one.

  • Core 30

    30 minIntensity 2 of 5

    Half an hour for abs, back and balance. Good on a lunch break.

  • Mobility Flow

    45 minIntensity 1 of 5

    Slow stretches and joint work. Loosen up after a hard week or a long day at a desk.
