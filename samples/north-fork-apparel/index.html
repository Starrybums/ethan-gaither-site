02 Story

A short list, done right.

The rule at North Fork

Heavy fabric. Plain cut.

We started with a jacket. We wanted one that took rain and a bad day without falling apart. Nothing on the shelf did, so we cut our own.

The tee, the flannel and the cap came next. Same rule for each of them: heavy fabric, a plain cut and stitching that holds.

We make in small runs. When a piece sells out, it's gone until the next run. We'd rather make less and have it last.

The name comes from a fork in a trail. Take the longer branch and you'll see more.
