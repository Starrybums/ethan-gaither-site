    Only the pages you need.

    Home, menu, hours, a place to find you. We agree the list before I start, so nothing shows up that you didn't ask for.

  4. Contact form

    A form people finish.

    A few short fields that go straight to your inbox. I test it before launch, and I test what happens when it can't send.

  5. Tap to call

    One tap to call.

    On a phone your number is a button. Nobody should have to copy and paste it to reach you.

  6. The map

    Your address, your hours and a directions link. If you go to your customers, it lists the towns you cover instead.

  7. Speed

    Quick on a bad connection.

    No heavy frameworks and no tracking scripts. Images are sized for the screen they're on. I run Lighthouse before launch.

    Now the same site, on a phone.

    Most people will find you on their phone. So I design for that screen first, then scale up.

  9. Up close

    Big tap targets, readable text and nothing to scroll sideways. This is the real mobile layout, not the desktop page squeezed down.
